# Sponsoring Girder

Girder is independently developed. Sponsorship funds:

- context-efficiency research (giving agents exactly the code they need
  instead of whole files),
- correctness of the semantic graph,
- integrations with coding agents,
- reproducible benchmarks, including published failures and limits,
- maintenance and releases.

Sponsor at <https://github.com/sponsors/dhishwasher>.

The tiers below are suggestions for the GitHub Sponsors profile. Check the
profile for current availability; checkout requires an active listing.

## Goal

**$1,000/month: fund ongoing maintenance, releases, benchmarks, and continued context-efficiency work on Girder.**

## Suggested monthly tiers

| Amount | Tier | Opt-in heading in [`SPONSORS.md`](../SPONSORS.md) |
|---|---|---|
| $5 | Supporter | Individual supporters |
| $15 | Builder | Individual supporters |
| $50 | Backer | Backers |
| $250 | Company Sponsor | Company sponsors |
| $1,000 | Founding Sponsor | Founding sponsors |

Benefits are lightweight: opt-in recognition under the heading above for
active monthly support, public updates when available, and thanks.

## Suggested one-time amounts

$10, $25, $100, $500. One-time sponsors get a thank-you only, with no
recurring recognition or support obligation.

## What sponsorship does not buy

Sponsorship does **not** buy guaranteed feature requests, private support,
SLAs, or control over the roadmap. It grants no special permissions; Girder's
terms are those of the [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE) (Apache License 2.0), which
already allows commercial reuse, forks, and hosted use for everyone.
